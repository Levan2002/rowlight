Rowlight

Pattern tracker & row counter

Never lose your place in a pattern again. Import a PDF or photo of your crochet, knitting or cross-stitch pattern, and a highlighter moves one row every time you tap the counter.

Your patterns stay on your iPhone. Rowlight has no accounts and no servers. It never uploads, shares, converts or trains on your patterns, so designers keep their work and you keep your privacy.

What it does

  • Highlighter row tracker – a bar sits on the row you're working. Tap + and it moves to the next row; charts can go bottom-up. Drag it, resize it, or tap the pattern to move it. Focus mode dims everything else.
  • Row counter with linked counters – stitch counters, repeat counters ("row 3 of 8") that follow your rows, undo, and a big left- or right-handed button.
  • Row reminders – "At row 24: start the decreases." Rowlight tells you when you get there.
  • Step checklist – turn pattern text into a checklist of rows and rounds, with stitch counts. The designer's wording stays exactly as written.
  • Markup – highlight finished stitches on cross-stitch charts, circle changes, write notes with your finger or Apple Pencil.
  • Lock Screen counter and Siri – count from the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, or say "Hey Siri, next row in Rowlight" with your hands full.
  • Craft tools – increase or decrease evenly, gauge calculator, US ⇄ UK crochet terms converter, hook, needle and yarn weight charts.

Help & questions

How do I get my pattern into Rowlight?

Tap + in Projects and choose PDF from Files, Photos or Screenshots, or Scan Paper Pattern. You can also share a PDF to Rowlight from Files, Safari, Mail or a shop's download page. The original file is copied, never changed.

How do I fit the highlighter to one row?

Tap the pattern where your row is, then drag the bar's bottom edge (or use Highlighter → Bar height) until it covers exactly one row. From then on every tap on + moves it one row. For charts that are read from the bottom up, choose Highlighter → Direction → Up.

Does it work for cross-stitch charts?

Yes. Use the highlighter for the row you're stitching, and the marker in Markup to colour in finished stitches. Rowlight doesn't read chart symbols; it keeps your place and your progress on the chart as it is.

What's free and what's in Rowlight Pro?

Everything is free for up to 3 projects (the two included patterns don't count), with one extra counter per project. Rowlight Pro adds unlimited projects and counters and the Lock Screen counter. Pro is a yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, with Family Sharing.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Are my patterns backed up?

Your projects are part of your normal iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). They aren't synced anywhere else.

Is Rowlight related to Pattern Keeper, Loopsy, knitCompanion or Markup R-XP?

No, Rowlight is an independent app. If you're on iPhone and looking for a simple, private way to keep your place in PDF patterns, Rowlight is built for exactly that.
