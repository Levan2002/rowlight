Privacy Policy

Rowlight · updated 30 September 2026
Short version: your patterns, notes, markup and counters stay on your iPhone. Rowlight has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your patterns and projects

PDFs, photos and scans you import, and everything you add to a project (counters, reminders, steps, notes, markup, time worked), are stored only in Rowlight's storage on your device. They are included in your own iPhone backup, and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Text recognition on scanned pages uses Apple's on-device frameworks.

Purchases

Rowlight Pro is sold through the App Store. To deliver and restore Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives your App Store purchase history for Rowlight together with a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app. This data isn't linked to your name, email or identity, and isn't used for tracking or advertising. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

In App Store terms, the data collected is: Purchase History and User ID, used for App Functionality, not linked to you, not used for tracking.

Camera and Siri

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a paper pattern, and scans stay on your device. Siri and Shortcuts requests are handled by Apple; Rowlight only receives the action (for example "next row").

Children

Rowlight doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a project in Rowlight to remove its copy of the pattern and everything you added. Deleting the app removes all of it.
